Source: National Programme for Prevention and Control of Non-Communicable Diseases, MoHFW, Government of India.

## [N1] Population-based screening

Population-based screening for hypertension, diabetes, and the three common cancers is offered to all individuals aged 30 years and above. Screening is delivered through Health and Wellness Centres using the Community Based Assessment Checklist administered by the ASHA.

## [N2] Diagnostic thresholds for hypertension

Hypertension is diagnosed when systolic blood pressure is 140 mmHg or higher, or diastolic blood pressure is 90 mmHg or higher, on two separate occasions. A single reading of 140/90 mmHg or above is a screen-positive result requiring confirmation, not a diagnosis.

## [N3] Diagnostic thresholds for diabetes

Diabetes is diagnosed at fasting plasma glucose of 126 mg/dL or higher, or two-hour post-glucose-load plasma glucose of 200 mg/dL or higher, or HbA1c of 6.5 percent or higher. A random blood glucose of 200 mg/dL or higher with classical symptoms is also diagnostic. Random capillary blood glucose of 140 mg/dL or above at screening is screen-positive and requires confirmatory testing.

## [N4] Referral criteria

Refer to a higher facility for blood pressure of 180/110 mmHg or above, any evidence of target organ damage, suspected secondary hypertension, blood glucose above 300 mg/dL, diabetic foot ulcer, or failure to reach control targets on maximal facility-level therapy.

## [N5] Treatment initiation at HWC level

First-line management is lifestyle modification for all screen-positive individuals. Pharmacological therapy for hypertension at the Health and Wellness Centre level follows the state standard treatment protocol and is typically initiated with a single agent, with the medical officer reviewing at four weeks.

## [N6] Follow-up intervals

Individuals on treatment with controlled parameters are followed up monthly for drug refill at the Health and Wellness Centre and reviewed by the medical officer at least once every three months. Screen-negative individuals aged 30 and above are rescreened annually.

## [N7] Recording and reporting

All screening, diagnosis and follow-up is recorded in the NCD application and the individual's ABHA-linked record where available.
