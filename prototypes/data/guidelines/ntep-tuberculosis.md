# National TB Elimination Programme (NTEP) — CONDENSED TEACHING EXTRACT

> **DEMONSTRATION DATA.** Condensed extract prepared for a CME teaching demo.
> Not a substitute for current NTEP technical and operational guidelines.
> Verify against the latest Central TB Division documents before any clinical use.

Source: Central TB Division, Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India.

## [T1] Presumptive TB definition

A person with any of: cough of two weeks or more, fever of two weeks or more, significant unintentional weight loss, haemoptysis, or enlarged cervical lymph nodes, is a presumptive pulmonary TB case and must be evaluated.

## [T2] Diagnostic algorithm

Upfront nucleic acid amplification testing is the recommended first diagnostic test for all presumptive TB cases. Chest X-ray is used as a screening and triage tool. Sputum smear microscopy is retained for treatment follow-up. Every microbiologically confirmed case must have universal drug susceptibility testing.

## [T3] Treatment regimen for drug-sensitive TB

Drug-sensitive TB is treated with a daily fixed-dose combination regimen. The intensive phase is two months of isoniazid, rifampicin, pyrazinamide and ethambutol. The continuation phase is four months of isoniazid, rifampicin and ethambutol. Dosing is weight-band based.

## [T4] Extension of continuation phase

The continuation phase may be extended by three months in patients with TB of the spine with neurological involvement, TB meningitis, and disseminated TB, on the treating physician's decision.

## [T5] Follow-up and outcome definitions

Follow-up sputum examination is done at the end of the intensive phase and at the end of treatment. Cure is defined as a microbiologically confirmed case who is smear or culture negative at the end of treatment. Treatment failure is a patient whose sputum remains or becomes positive at four months or later.

## [T6] Contact tracing and preventive therapy

All household contacts of a pulmonary TB patient must be screened. TB preventive therapy is offered to eligible contacts after active TB is ruled out, including children under five years and people living with HIV.

## [T7] Nutritional support

Nikshay Poshan Yojana provides direct benefit transfer for nutritional support to every notified TB patient for the duration of treatment.
